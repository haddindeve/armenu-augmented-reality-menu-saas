# ARMenu - Augmented Reality Restaurant Menu SaaS - frequently asked questions

## Do diners need an app?

No - AR runs in the phone's browser from a QR code or link.

## How do restaurants add dishes?

Through a management interface where each dish can carry a 3D model alongside its price and description.

## Is it multi-tenant?

Yes - each restaurant manages its own menu within one platform.
